PIANO BY EAR
============

There is no screen. You use it only from your piano keyboard.

WHAT YOU NEED
- A MIDI keyboard plugged into this computer by USB. Any size works, and 61
  or 88 keys is best.
- If your keyboard makes its own sound (a digital piano), that is the sound
  you will hear. Nothing else to do.
- If your keyboard makes NO sound of its own (a controller), download the
  pianos once (below), and use speakers or headphones on the computer.

START
- Mac: open "Piano by Ear" from Applications. Click the app again for the
  sound, restart, or quit.
- Windows: double-click "Piano by Ear.cmd". Leave the black window open and
  close it to stop.
- When it hears your keyboard, it clicks once. Play a note.

WHO IS PLAYING
- Play ONE note and you are a guest. Nothing is kept, and that note is your
  anchor (below): the session starts there.
- Play a CHORD that is yours (any notes, held together, then let go) and
  that chord becomes your login. Play the same notes in the same octave next
  time and your history comes back. Pick something nobody else in the house
  will play by accident.
- After the chord you hear a low-high click. Now play ONE NOTE, YOUR ANCHOR.
  Any note will do. The session starts from it and it becomes the first
  key. Nothing happens until you play it.
- From then on, every question starts from the last note you played, so
  leave your hand where it lands.
- Beginners start on the white keys. The black keys come in as you earn
  them.
- A new history is kept only after you have answered 20 questions. One
  nobody has played for 30 days is put away.

THE DRILL
- You hear a note or a short phrase. Play it back. That is the whole game.
- Keep to the metronome. Start your answer on any click after the call ends.
- It starts simple and gets harder only as you get things right. A missed
  phrase comes back.
- After each phrase the metronome stops for a couple of seconds. If you
  think you missed a note, play it then. If not, play nothing.
- There are no beeps, chimes or error sounds. Every note you hear is one
  you are being asked to play back.
- It ends by itself after about 20 minutes, on something you got right.
  Ten seconds of silence also ends it. Play your chord or a note to go again.

TIPS
- Don't look at your hands. Listen, and let your hand find the note.
- Short daily sittings beat long weekly ones.

IF THE METRONOME SOUNDS LIKE A PIANO NOTE (OR NOT AT ALL)
Some keyboards (Kawai, and other pianos without drum sounds) play the
metronome as a note. Have the computer play it instead:
- Mac: click the app while it is running and choose "Metronome from the
  computer".
- Windows: double-click "Switch metronome.cmd", then start Piano by Ear.
Turn the computer's sound on to hear it.

A KEYBOARD WITH NO SOUND OF ITS OWN
- Mac: start the app, click it again, and choose "Download pianos".
- Windows: double-click "Download pianos.cmd", then start Piano by Ear.
It downloads about 75 MB, once. To go back to the keyboard's own sound:
on a Mac choose "Use my keyboard's own sound" from the same menu; on
Windows double-click "Use keyboard sound.cmd".

FREE PLAY (with the downloaded pianos)
- Mac: click the app while it is running and choose "Free play".
- Windows: double-click "Free play.cmd" instead of Piano by Ear.
Your keyboard plays the grand piano. Nothing is graded.

WHERE YOUR HISTORY LIVES
In a folder called .piano-by-ear in your home folder. Deleting it starts
everyone over.
