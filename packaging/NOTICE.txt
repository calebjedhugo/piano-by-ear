MUSIC (app/corpus/)
- phrases.json and poly.json are excerpts made from Craig Stuart Sapp's
  Humdrum editions of the Bach 371 chorales and the Mozart piano sonatas:
    https://github.com/craigsapp/bach-370-chorales
    https://github.com/craigsapp/mozart-piano-sonatas
  Those editions are licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, and so are these files:
    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/
  They may not be sold.
- trios.json is made from Bach as typeset at the Mutopia Project
  (https://www.mutopiaproject.org) by Olivier Vermersch, Davide Castellone,
  Jarle Fagerheim and Urs Metzger. Every source file is marked public
  domain by its typesetter.
- hymns.json holds hymn tunes published before 1930, encoded by Caleb Hugo.
